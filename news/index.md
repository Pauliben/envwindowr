# Changelog

## envwindowr 0.1.0

- Initial GitHub-ready package.
- User-supplied daily weather, traits, and environment-specific periods.
- Fixed or multiple window sizes and configurable scan steps.
- Mean, sum, minimum, maximum, median, standard deviation, or custom
  summaries.
- Pearson, Spearman, and Kendall correlation methods.
- Weather coverage checks, explicit status codes, adjusted p-values,
  rankings, diagnostics, and plots.
- Simulated data with a known environmental signal.
